14. Situation Intelligence
Aivira should not only wait for exact commands. It should understand context and suggest useful actions, while asking
permission before acting.
School fee
Situation: School fee is due in five days.
Aivira suggestion: Rohan's school fee of ■8,000 is due on 15 December. Would you like me to remind you on 12
December?
Delivery executive
Situation: The user is riding and receives an important call.
Aivira suggestion: Your delivery manager is calling. You can answer using your headset or call back when safe.
Startup founder
Situation: An investor follow-up is due today.
Aivira suggestion: You planned to send the updated pitch deck to your investor today. Would you like me to prepare an
email draft?
Elderly parent
Situation: A scheduled medicine reminder remains unconfirmed.
Aivira suggestion: Papa's 9 PM medicine reminder hasn't been confirmed. Would you like to send a check-in
notification?

17. Recommended Product Architecture
Build a Personal + Family + Organisation workspace system.
Multiple workspaces
The same person can manage personal, family and business responsibilities without
needing separate apps or accounts.
Workspace members
Each workspace can have its own members, tasks, reminders and shared
information.
Permissions
Control who can view, edit, assign or approve information and actions.
Child accounts
Provide age-appropriate experiences with parental supervision and consent.
Business separation
Keep business information separate from personal and family information.
Final Product Vision
Aivira
Not just an assistant. An AI that manages life's responsibilities.
From remembering a child's school fees to helping a company owner manage hundreds of responsibilities.
One platform. Multiple personas. Intelligent assistance.
Key recommendation: Make Situation Intelligence, Promise Tracker and Smart Escalation the three signature features of Aivira.
Voice assistants can take commands, but Aivira should understand what matters, remember commitments and help people avoid
forgetting important things.
Safety note: Automated actions should remain permission-based, with special safeguards for children, financial activity and driving.
Driving features should prioritise hands-free use and never encourage phone interaction while riding.
