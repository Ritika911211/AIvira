Aivira
Updated Safety Agent – Integrated Feature Addendum
This update expands the original DriveSafe and SafeCircle concepts into one intelligent safety module.
Updated module name: Aivira Safety Agent
One safety engine with specialised modes for women, delivery riders, drivers, walkers, travellers, families and elderly users. It
combines journey check-ins, hands-free assistance, trusted contacts and permission-based emergency workflows.
1. Women's Safety Assistant
A set of consent-based tools for women travelling alone, commuting or feeling unsafe.
1. Live Location Sharing
Share real-time location with selected trusted contacts during a journey, with the user's
permission.
2. Safe Journey Timer
Set an expected arrival time and request a safety check-in at the destination.
3. SOS Emergency Activation
Send an emergency alert and available location information to selected trusted contacts.
4. Route Deviation Alert
Detect a significant unexpected route change where location access and suitable
detection are available; ask the user to confirm they are safe.
5. Emergency Calling
Offer quick access to trusted contacts or configured emergency services.
6. Missed Check-in Alert
Notify selected trusted contacts if a configured check-in is missed.
7. Voice-Activated SOS
Allow a configured voice command to start the emergency workflow where supported.
8. Hands-Free Safety Mode
Support voice interaction through compatible headsets while walking or travelling.
9. Trusted Circle
Create a private group of family members or friends for journey updates.
10. Discreet Emergency Shortcut
Provide a quick, discreet way to trigger an alert without navigating multiple screens.
2. Smart Real-Life Safety Scenarios
A. Woman travelling alone at night
The user starts Safe Journey Mode, selects a destination and trusted contact, shares location with permission, and sets an
arrival timer. If the check-in is missed, Aivira follows the user's configured alert and escalation rules.
B. Unexpected route deviation
Aivira detects a significant deviation from the planned route where supported, asks whether everything is okay, and offers
options to contact a trusted person or activate SOS. A route change alone is not treated as proof of danger.
C. Delivery rider receives an urgent call
Aivira announces the caller, supports compatible hands-free call controls, offers an approved driving auto-reply if unanswered,
and reminds the rider to call back when safe.
D. User feels unsafe
The user triggers SOS through a configured voice command or discreet control. Aivira alerts selected trusted contacts, shares
available location information and offers emergency calling.

5. Core Differentiator: AI Safety Check-in Intelligence
Aivira should understand the journey context rather than send only generic notifications. A user might say: “I'm going to college;
I'll reach home by 7 PM”, “I'm travelling alone to the railway station” or “I'm delivering an order in an unfamiliar area.” Aivira can
propose an appropriate check-in schedule, keep authorised contacts informed and offer help if a check-in is missed.
6. Integration with the Original Catalogue
Original DriveSafe Assistant
Its driving mode, urgent call alerts, hands-free call handling, priority contacts, driving
auto-reply, voice notifications and delivery instructions are retained as the DriveSafe
mode.
Original SafeCircle
Its SOS alert, emergency contacts, live location sharing, safe-arrival check-in, travel
check-in and missed check-in alerts are incorporated into Aivira Safety Agent.
Updated structure
Aivira Safety Agent becomes the umbrella module. DriveSafe and SafeCircle
capabilities become specialised modes rather than disconnected modules.
7. Safety, Privacy & Implementation Requirements
Consent-based location access
Explain when location is shared, with whom, and for how long. Let users stop sharing.
No false certainty
A route deviation or missed check-in is a signal to check, not proof of an emergency.
Configurable escalation
Users choose trusted contacts, timing, retries and what happens after a missed
check-in.
Hands-free driving safety
Do not encourage phone interaction while riding or driving. Use compatible hands-free
controls and follow platform restrictions.
Emergency service availability
Do not promise automatic rescue or guaranteed emergency-service connection;
availability depends on device, network, region and integrations.
Child and vulnerable-user safeguards
Use age-appropriate controls, authorised guardians and careful permission
management.
Data protection
Protect location history and emergency information with strong access controls and
retention settings.
